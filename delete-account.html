Say that you want a TrailGrid deletion review, whether you still have the app, and whether the request concerns purchaser data, shared missions or local records. Do not send GPS histories, passwords, invitations, purchase/session tokens, private keys or card details.

What happens next

The link opens your mail app; nothing is sent until you send it. Opening this page does not submit a request. JLJames will establish scope and arrange a secure ownership check. This page requests no government-ID upload or payment details. Hosted lost-device verification and large-account fulfillment remain to be qualified before public launch.

Purchaser fulfillment is locally tested but not deployed. A recorded request is not completed deletion. Support must confirm the outcome, explain unresolved processing and identify retained records. This pre-release page promises neither an automatic deadline nor a completed production deletion service.

Purchases and retained records

A request does not itself cancel Plus. Cancel renewal in Google Play; support can explain the steps. Active benefits, unused passes and pending acknowledgment/consumption require resolution without silent loss of paid value. Large accounts require bounded review. These are follow-up steps, not an indefinite exemption from deletion.

Eligible fulfillment removes purchaser/device associations, pending login secrets, encrypted purchase tokens and associated commercial records. Minimal hashed token/refund-order anti-replay records and a completion reference remain for fraud prevention. Production retention periods and support-mail handling are not finalized.

Local and shared evidence

Purchaser deletion does not erase GPS, grids, hazards, human reviews, offline maps, device authentication or independent teammate copies. Local Archive and Trash are recoverable; permanent local deletion requires the separate explicit app action.

State shared-cloud evidence or device-identity requests separately. Revocation, purchase unlinking and historical erasure differ. Shared erasure must consider other members, dependent history and backups. Automatic cloud cleanup is disabled; complete production erasure/retention procedures remain launch gates.
