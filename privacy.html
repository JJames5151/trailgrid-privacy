TRAILGRID / PRE-RELEASE INFORMATION

Privacy policy

How TrailGrid handles search history, optional sharing, purchases and privacy choices.

Prepared 2026-10-29 · current pre-release scope. Public app launch is pending.

The TrailGrid website now has this information at trailgridapp.com/privacy. This compatible page remains available for existing app and store links.

About TrailGrid

TrailGrid is the product brand. Its publisher is listed as JLJames in Google Play. This policy covers TrailGrid and the separate TrailGrid Staging application. It describes current pre-release behavior and identifies services that are not active. Live advertising and public app sales have not launched. Private Staging testing uses Google test advertisements and licensed test purchases.

Free solo searching is accountless. Creating or joining a shared search enrolls a device for authorized team access. Optional Google sign-in supports purchase ownership and recovery when the purchase service is enabled.

Location and search records

TrailGrid uses precise location to show your position, record deliberate searching, estimate coverage, map hazards and provide grid and hazard guidance. A search you start can continue collecting location in the background while the screen is locked or another app is visible. Pause keeps navigation and safety alerts active without crediting inspection coverage. End Search stops session recording. Locate Me and hazard mapping can also use location while the map is open.

Records include coordinates, reported accuracy, timestamps, recording mode, participant/device provenance and available motion information such as speed and bearing. Mission boundaries, immutable grids, hazards and revisions, human verification/reopening, settings and offline maps are stored locally. Simplified display trails do not replace original evidence. Coverage estimates do not certify that terrain is safe or fully searched.

Opening a saved mission does not start recording. Force-stop, power loss, permission changes or platform restrictions can interrupt GPS. TrailGrid cannot record while the phone is off.

Shared searches

Creating or joining a shared mission allows the service and authorized teammates to receive saved search routes, coverage evidence, boundaries, hazards, reviews and participant metadata. Names, assigned colors and stable identifiers support membership and attribution. Offline changes may upload when synchronization resumes.

Live-location sharing is separate from durable history. Turning it off stops new live-position sharing; it does not stop synchronization of saved mission evidence. Revocation prevents new authorized access but cannot erase information another phone already received.

Advertising and privacy choices

Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) may process IP addresses and IP-derived approximate location, advertising/app-set identifiers, device information, ad interactions and diagnostics under applicable choices and Google terms. Nonpersonalized advertising does not mean zero collection.

TrailGrid does not supply GPS observations, search boundaries, hazards, teammate names or search history as advertising targeting. It does not sell search histories. Banners are limited to Home and Saved Searches. Search maps, recording controls, safety warnings, verification, QR, permissions, recovery and purchase screens have no ads.

Advertising choices and location permissions are separate. Searching works when consent is denied or unavailable. Applicable Google privacy choices can be reopened in Settings. Test builds require adult confirmation before ad requests; unknown or under-18 selections request none. This is not verified age or a claim of children’s-program eligibility. Current candidates use test banners; live advertising is not active.

Purchases and optional Google identity

Google Play handles payments. TrailGrid does not receive payment-card details or Google passwords. Its purchaser ledger stores no Google email or profile name. When enabled, optional Google sign-in links a pseudonymous purchaser identifier to verified purchases, encrypted purchase tokens, product/order status, activation, expiration and refund evidence. Pseudonymous data is still personal data.

The ledger supports recovery of ownership, including a consumed pass that Play no longer lists. Genuine private-test purchases and same-device Restore have been tested. Full replacement-device history recovery remains a launch-acceptance requirement. A pass requires explicit mission activation. Personal ad removal belongs only to the purchaser, independently of team capacity. Free teammates need not buy anything to join an authorized mission. Cancel renewal separately in Google Play; requesting deletion does not cancel Plus.

Providers, security and backups

Cloudflare hosts the current team service. Access uses authenticated devices and mission membership, with encrypted transport. Team evidence is not end-to-end encrypted against the service operator. Device signing keys use the platform secure-key service. Operational alerts use resource/error aggregates rather than private GPS routes or purchase tokens.

Google Maps and offline-map providers receive map/network requests. Google documents Maps SDK collection of device/SDK metadata, IP address, crash information, a Maps-specific pseudonymous identifier and, depending on use, map interactions. Offline maps do not guarantee that every SDK is silent.

Android operating-system backup and device-transfer behavior depends on the build and platform. The prepared public profile disables Android application backup. Do not rely on operating-system transfer to recover mission history or secure keys. This is separate from team synchronization and optional personal cloud backup.

Cloudflare hosts TrailGridApp.com and processes website requests, including IP addresses and security information. GitHub Pages continues to serve the earlier policy and deletion URLs and may process requests made to those URLs. These pages contain no advertising, analytics, contact forms, third-party scripts or cookies set by TrailGrid. Domain contact addresses forward through Cloudflare to the existing Google-hosted support inbox; those providers process your address, message and correspondence so TrailGrid can respond. A normal reply may reveal the responding mailbox address. A public alias is not an anonymous messaging service.

Personal cloud backup — preparation, not a protection promise

Automatic encrypted personal cloud backup and authorized replacement-device history recovery are planned Plus benefits at $4.99/month. They are not yet accepted for public use. Free and Expedition-only users do not receive automatic personal backup. Local, unsent or unconfirmed changes are not protected by a server backup.

The prepared service protects complete mission evidence with authenticated purchaser/device access, encryption in transit and storage, integrity validation and conflict-safe recovery. It is operator-decryptable, not end-to-end encrypted against the operator. Team synchronization is separate and does not guarantee a complete personal backup.

The proposed cancellation treatment is a 30-day read-only recovery period after paid backup access ends. Storage, retention, deletion and replacement-device fulfillment must be approved and qualified before this service launches. Automatic backup deletion and the separate proposed 90-day shared-history policy are not active. Existing exported archives remain compatible; no new protection is claimed for them.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them. Archive and Trash retain recoverable data; permanent local deletion is separate. Local deletion does not erase server history or teammate copies. Use End Search, rather than deletion, to stop recording.

Automatic shared-record cleanup is disabled in the private test service. No automatic cloud-expiry period is promised. Shared evidence and dependent histories require verified, scoped review to avoid corrupting other participants’ records. Complete shared-cloud/lost-device erasure procedures remain public-launch requirements; contact TrailGrid about a specific test-data request.

Purchaser deletion has an authenticated request and locally tested fulfillment implementation. Complete hosted fulfillment and lost-device handling remain public-launch requirements. Eligible fulfillment removes purchaser/device links, pending login secrets, encrypted purchase tokens and associated commercial records. Minimal hashed token/refund-order anti-replay records and a completion reference remain for fraud prevention. They are pseudonymous, not completely anonymous. Production retention periods and support-mail handling require approval before public sales.

Active benefits, unused passes, unfinished store processing, stale store status or large record sets require supported review. They must not silently forfeit paid value or become a permanent refusal to process deletion. Purchaser deletion does not erase missions, device authentication or teammates’ independent evidence. See the deletion page for the current request path and limits.

Your choices and contact

You control Start, Pause, Resume and End; separate live sharing; local missions; available offline maps; and Android location/notification permissions. Optional camera access scans invitation QR codes. Bluetooth speech does not require microphone access.

Contact TrailGrid using the address below for support, access or deletion questions. Do not email GPS histories, passwords, invitations, purchase/session tokens, private keys or card details. Ownership and scope must be verified; an email address alone is not proof.

Material data-flow or SDK changes require policy and disclosure review. This pre-release policy does not claim that public-launch deletion, retention, consent or store-review gates have passed.
